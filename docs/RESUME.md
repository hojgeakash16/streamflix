## Interview questions you should be able to answer

1. **Why three tiers?** Separation of concerns: each tier scales, deploys and is secured independently. The DB is never exposed to the internet.
2. **Why is the DB in private subnets?** No route to an internet gateway and its security group only trusts the app tier, so it is unreachable from outside the VPC.
3. **Why CloudFront in front of both S3 and the ALB?** HTTPS, caching for static files, one domain (no CORS), and the ALB is only reached through it for the browser path.
4. **What does the Auto Scaling Group give you?** Self-healing (ELB health checks replace failed instances) and the ability to scale out to more instances.
5. **What would you change for production?** Put app servers in private subnets with a NAT gateway or VPC endpoints, RDS Multi-AZ, secrets in Secrets Manager, custom domain + ACM, WAF, remote Terraform state, CI/CD, and containers on ECS/Fargate.
6. **How are secrets handled today, and what is the weakness?** Generated by Terraform and injected through user-data; they sit in Terraform state and instance metadata, which is why Secrets Manager is the next upgrade.
7. **Why Terraform over clicking in the console?** Repeatable, reviewable in git, easy teardown, and no configuration drift.
